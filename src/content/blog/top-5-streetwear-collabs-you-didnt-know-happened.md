Streetwear collaborations are nothing new. From luxury fashion houses to fast-food chains, brands have joined forces with some pretty unexpected partners over the years. While everyone remembers the likes of Supreme x Louis Vuitton and Nike x Off-White, there are plenty of forgotten collaborations that deserve a little more attention.

Here are five streetwear collaborations you might not have known existed.

## 1. Supreme x Alpinestars (2007)

![Supreme x Alpinestars 2007 motorcycle jacket](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0773/1052/5595/files/supreme-x-alpinestars-2007-jacket.jpg?v=1795700702 "The Supreme x Alpinestars jacket, 2007. Image: Hypebeast")

Who would have thought that Supreme would venture into motorcycle apparel?

In 2007, Supreme teamed up with Italian motorcycle equipment manufacturer Alpinestars to create an extremely limited motorcycle jacket. The project also featured branding from other streetwear heavyweights, including UNDFTD, Stüssy and Vans Syndicate, giving it the appearance of a fully sponsored racing jacket.

Here’s the interesting part: only 11 jackets were reportedly produced in Italy, making this one of the rarest Supreme collaborations ever.

## 2. Palace x Polo Ralph Lauren (2018)

![Palace x Polo Ralph Lauren 2018 Polo Bear kickflip sweater](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0773/1052/5595/files/palace-x-polo-ralph-lauren-2018-polo-bear-sweater.jpg?v=1795700704 "The Polo Bear kickflip sweater. Image: Hypebae"){full}

Skateboarding culture meets American preppy fashion.

In 2018, British skateboarding label Palace joined forces with Polo Ralph Lauren for a collaboration that brought together two very different fashion identities.

The collection featured Polo’s iconic teddy bear reimagined with Palace’s skateboarding aesthetic, alongside tartan pieces, rugby shirts, outerwear and accessories. One particularly memorable piece was a knit sweater featuring the Polo Bear performing a kickflip.

It was an unexpected partnership that brought together the worlds of skateboarding and traditional American fashion.

## 3. A.P.C. x Carhartt WIP (2020)

![A.P.C. x Carhartt WIP Michigan Chore Coat](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0773/1052/5595/files/apc-x-carhartt-wip-michigan-chore-coat.jpg?v=1795700707 "The Michigan Chore Coat, reworked. Image: Novelship")

Minimalism meets workwear.

French minimalist label A.P.C. and American workwear-inspired brand Carhartt WIP might seem like an unlikely pairing, but their collaboration made plenty of sense.

Their 2020 capsule collection reinterpreted classic Carhartt silhouettes through A.P.C.’s cleaner and more refined design language. One standout piece was the iconic Michigan Chore Coat, reworked using sleeker cotton fabrics instead of its traditional rugged canvas.

The collection also included hoodies, sweatshirts, denim, bags and accessories, blending everyday workwear functionality with understated Parisian aesthetics.

## 4. The North Face x Brain Dead (2019)

![The North Face x Brain Dead Denali fleece jacket](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0773/1052/5595/files/the-north-face-x-brain-dead-denali-fleece.jpg?v=1795700709 "The Brain Dead Denali fleece. Image: StockX"){full}

Outdoor functionality meets psychedelic streetwear.

Los Angeles-based creative collective Brain Dead brought its unconventional visual identity to The North Face in a collaboration that was anything but ordinary.

Drawing inspiration from the counterculture movements of 1960s and 1970s California, the collection reimagined The North Face’s classic Denali, Nuptse and Mountain Jackets with psychedelic graphics, unusual colour combinations and handcrafted-looking details.

Rather than simply placing two logos on existing products, the collaboration offered a completely different interpretation of traditional outdoor apparel.

## 5. Supreme x FILA (2007)

![Supreme x FILA 2007 track jacket, World Famous Supreme Team back print](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0773/1052/5595/files/supreme-x-fila-2007-track-jacket.jpg?v=1795700711 "The World Famous Supreme Team back print. Image: Etsy")

Before retro sportswear became fashionable again, Supreme had already teamed up with Italian sportswear brand FILA.

Their 2007 collaboration featured a vintage-inspired capsule consisting of headbands, wristbands, a short-sleeved polo shirt and a track jacket.

The standout piece featured Supreme’s iconic World Famous Supreme Team branding across the back, combining FILA’s nostalgic sporting aesthetic with Supreme’s New York streetwear identity.

Interestingly, Supreme also created its own version of the FILA Fitness sneaker back in 2002, although that particular sample never made it to retail.

## Final Thoughts

What makes these collaborations interesting isn’t necessarily how popular they became, but how unexpected some of the partnerships were.

Streetwear has always been about experimentation, creativity and bringing different cultures together. Sometimes, the most interesting collaborations aren’t the ones everyone talks about, but the ones you stumble across years later and wonder how you missed them in the first place.

After all, that’s part of the fun of streetwear.
